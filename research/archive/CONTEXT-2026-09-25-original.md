## Initial Spain-specific planning recommendations

Checked against official sources on 2026-09-25; recheck before booking because visa instructions, schedules, hours, and prices can change.

- **Protect enough time for all three cities and the relaxed pace.** The return flight leaves Spain on Feb 3, so do not treat that date as a Spain sightseeing day. Count backward from that flight when setting the route and hotel nights. Prefer Jan 25 outbound to maximize time. If the resulting stay is short, keep the three-city requirement but reduce the number of timed attractions per day and clearly flag the pace tradeoff.
- **Keep the route open until the long-haul airports are compared.** A workable rail-first outline is Barcelona, then Seville, then Granada (or the reverse); Renfe currently lists Barcelona–Seville service and an Andalusian Granada–Córdoba–Seville corridor. Madrid may be used for flights or connections, but do not add it as a sightseeing or overnight stop. The actual 2027 timetable, travel time, fares, and final airport should determine the order. Avoid booking a same-day international departure after a separate train connection.
- **Keep Córdoba out of the first draft.** The user wants all three selected cities and a relaxed pace; only consider Córdoba if an unexpectedly generous flight schedule leaves a spare day.
- **Start visa appointments and document preparation now.** Both consular pages currently allow applications from 6 months to 15 days before travel; the Hong Kong page warns that appointments may fill, and Beijing applicants book through BLS. For a Jan 25–27, 2027 trip, the application window is already open. Spain should be the only or main Schengen destination for applying through Spain. The Hong Kong office accepts applications from residents of its consular district; being physically in Hong Kong alone may not be sufficient. Its checklist asks for proof of residence and full-trip accommodation, and advises applicants not to pay for flights before visa approval. Confirm whether each office accepts an unpaid/refundable flight itinerary for the visa file, and avoid non-refundable purchases while visas are pending. Recheck the complete, location-specific checklist before submitting.
- **Prioritize the Alhambra date once the route is stable.** The official Patronato advises advance online purchase because capacity is limited. The ticket is nominative; carry the original passport/ID, and observe the timed Nasrid Palaces entry. Purchase only through the [official Alhambra ticket site](https://tickets.alhambra-patronato.es/).
- **Treat Sagrada Família as another advance-ticket item.** Its official FAQ currently says tickets are generally released about two months ahead, are nominative, and require photo ID at entry. Check the official [ticket page](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/tickets) when the 2027 dates approach. For Seville, check availability and hours on the [official Alcázar visitor page](https://alcazarsevilla.org/prepara-la-visita/).
- **Plan for winter daylight and weather.** Use layers and a light waterproof shell, leave some flexibility for rain/cold, and avoid packing every day with outdoor plans. Check the city-specific forecast shortly before departure.

## Research and booking notes

These are leads from the GitHub research in this project, **not installed or selected integrations**. Their live services and purchase flows have not been tested.

- **Itinerary planning:** [trip-planner-skill](https://github.com/skywain/trip-planner-skill) can create a detailed itinerary with current opening-hour/price checks and booking links; its instructions explicitly leave booking and payment to the traveler.
- **Itinerary organization:** [wanderlog-mcp](https://github.com/shaikhspeare/wanderlog-mcp) can create/edit a Wanderlog trip and add places, hotel entries, notes, and checklists. It is an unofficial integration using Wanderlog's private web API and a session cookie.
- **Hotel booking:** [TourMind Hotel Booking AI MCP](https://github.com/tourmind-com/Hotel-Booking-AI-MCP) documents a hosted MCP plus companion Skill; search is public, while order operations require a `user_key`. The repo contains the product contract/Skill, not the server implementation. [DIDA's global](https://github.com/DIDA-AI/Dida-Hotel-MCP-Global) and [China](https://github.com/DIDA-AI/Dida-hotel-MCP-CN) MCP repos document hotel search; their self-service API-key mode has fewer tools than the OAuth business mode that includes booking/payment. [WinWin.travel](https://github.com/WinWin-travel/MCP-server) and [1Stay](https://github.com/STAYKER-COM/1Stay-mcp) describe hosted hotel reservation flows with external checkout links.
- **Homestay search:** [StayingAPI Hotel MCP](https://github.com/stayingapi/hotel-mcp) searches/computes prices across Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, and Google Hotels, but its listed tools are read-only. A separate [Airbnb MCP](https://github.com/markswendsen-code/mcp-airbnb) claims browser-based booking; it stores Airbnb session cookies and uses browser automation, so do not treat it as a trusted default.
- **Attractions and tickets:** [GetYourGuide MCP](https://github.com/chrischall/getyourguide-mcp), [Viator MCP](https://github.com/chrischall/viator-mcp), and [TicketLens MCP](https://github.com/ticketlens/ticketlens-experiences-mcp) focus on search, details, or availability; their documented tools do not complete standard ticket purchases. [Last Minute Deals](https://github.com/OyaAIProd/lastminutedeals-api) documents checkout/booking for a narrower last-minute activity inventory and should be verified before relying on it.

When comparing integrations, distinguish live search/availability from reservation creation and payment. Recheck prices, cancellation terms, and ticket availability against the provider at the time of booking. For this trip, include only hotel/homestay rates that explicitly provide free cancellation through a stated deadline; if none fit the budget, report that constraint and ask before considering any exception. Never make a non-refundable accommodation booking under the current preference.

## Project fit and due-diligence ranking

**Snapshot: 2026-09-25.** Rankings are by role because itinerary planning, trip storage, inventory search, and checkout are different jobs. I weigh fit for this trip first, then provider/source reliability and transaction clarity, then recent maintenance; stars/forks are a small community signal only. GitHub metadata was refreshed on this date. No live MCP calls, bookings, or payments were tested, so these are research rankings rather than service guarantees.

### Trip planning and itinerary storage

1. **[trip-planner-skill](https://github.com/skywain/trip-planner-skill)** — best planning fit for a 2027 Barcelona–Granada–Seville trip: route and day planning, date/price/opening-hour checks, visa research from official sources, flight comparisons, hotel shortlists, and booking links. **40 stars; last pushed 2026-09-05; MIT.** It does not book or pay.
2. **[wanderlog-mcp](https://github.com/shaikhspeare/wanderlog-mcp)** — best optional itinerary write-back: **137 stars / 56 forks; last pushed 2026-09-14; MIT.** README shows tests/coverage signals. Reliability is conditional because it uses Wanderlog's private web-client API and a login session cookie; use only if comfortable with that dependency.

### Hotels

1. **[TourMind Hotel Booking AI](https://github.com/tourmind-com/Hotel-Booking-AI) + [its MCP package](https://github.com/tourmind-com/Hotel-Booking-AI-MCP)** — closest personal-use booking workflow: live search/rates and documented order/payment operations. The Skill repo has **6 stars** and the MCP package **0 stars**; both were last pushed **2026-09-09**. The service is hosted and the MCP repo contains a Skill/contract rather than server source; order operations need a `user_key`. High functional fit, but lower independent verification.
2. **[DIDA Global MCP](https://github.com/DIDA-AI/Dida-Hotel-MCP-Global) / [DIDA China MCP](https://github.com/DIDA-AI/Dida-hotel-MCP-CN)** — strongest hotel supplier provenance among these options: README identifies the service as DIDA/RollingGo's own offering. Global: **82 stars, last pushed 2026-09-08, MIT**; China: **10 stars, last pushed 2026-08-26, MIT**. The self-service API-key mode documents search/details/tags; booking and payment tools are in the OAuth business mode, so individual access to actual checkout is a major limitation.
3. **[WinWin.travel MCP](https://github.com/WinWin-travel/MCP-server)** — search, reservation creation, payment link, and status check. **11 stars; last pushed 2026-08-25; MIT.** A viable hosted alternative, but the service and inventory are vendor-operated and have not been independently tested here.
4. **[1Stay MCP](https://github.com/STAYKER-COM/1Stay-mcp)** — search, booking checkout, lookup, and cancellation. **3 stars; last pushed 2026-09-23; GitHub did not identify an SPDX license.** Recent activity is positive, but there is little community validation.

**Keep the TourMind business product separate:** [Tourmind-Booking-Skills](https://github.com/tourmind-com/Tourmind-Booking-Skills) has **1,257 stars / 206 forks**, was last pushed **2026-09-24**, and declares MIT; however, it requires a business Skill Token. Those signals do not validate the personal-use TourMind MCP endpoint above.

### Homestays

1. **[StayingAPI Hotel MCP](https://github.com/stayingapi/hotel-mcp)** — best of these for Airbnb/Vrbo/Booking.com comparison; **1 star; last pushed 2026-07-21; MIT.** Its documented tools are read-only and use the provider's credit balance, so booking still happens elsewhere.
2. **[mcp-airbnb](https://github.com/markswendsen-code/mcp-airbnb)** — the README claims browser-based booking, but this is the weakest reliability choice: **0 stars; last pushed 2026-03-16; no license identified**; it stores Airbnb session cookies and uses browser automation/anti-bot stealth. Do not use it as the default booking route.

### Attraction tickets and activities

1. **[GetYourGuide MCP](https://github.com/chrischall/getyourguide-mcp)** — best first lookup for activities: partner API search, options, and availability; **0 stars; last pushed 2026-09-23; MIT.** Read-only; maintainer says it is AI-developed/maintained, so validate results at the provider.
2. **[Viator MCP](https://github.com/chrischall/viator-mcp)** — similar search/availability and booking links; **1 star; last pushed 2026-09-23; no license identified**; also self-described as AI-developed. Read-only.
3. **[TicketLens MCP](https://github.com/ticketlens/ticketlens-experiences-mcp)** — wider ticket/activity search; **1 star; last pushed 2026-09-12; Apache-2.0.** No documented purchase/checkout tool and little community validation.
4. **[Last Minute Deals](https://github.com/OyaAIProd/lastminutedeals-api)** — documents a real activity checkout flow, but **0 stars; last pushed 2026-04-26; MIT**, and inventory is for last-minute availability. Poor fit for planning this January/February trip in advance; reconsider only near travel if the service is active.

### Practical shortlist for this trip

- Use **trip-planner-skill** as the planning layer; compare the Beijing and Hong Kong flight rendezvous options on total group cost once the outbound date is selected.
- For hotel research, compare **TourMind personal MCP** (more direct consumer booking flow) with **DIDA** (better provider provenance, but transaction access is business-gated). Do not treat search results as confirmed reservations.
